2. No Investment Advice

Nothing on this platform constitutes investment advice, a recommendation to buy or sell any security, or an endorsement of any financial instrument. The PCRS (Physical Climate Risk Score), pricing gap analysis, and greenwash flags are research signals for due diligence purposes only. Users must conduct their own independent analysis before making any investment decisions.

3. Data Accuracy & Limitations

  • Bond data: Sourced from Climate Bonds Initiative (CBI) public registry. Some bond GPS coordinates use country centroid approximations rather than exact project locations.
  • Climate hazard data: Sourced from World Bank APIs and NASA. Indices are normalised approximations, not hydrological engineering models.
  • PCRS model: Trained on synthetic hazard-derived labels. Not backtested against historical bond default events due to proprietary data limitations (Munich Re / Swiss Re).
  • Satellite imagery: ESA Sentinel-2 (2015+ only). Pre-2015 bonds cannot be satellite-verified. Cloud cover and seasonal variation may affect NDVI accuracy.
  • Yield data: Yahoo Finance — unofficial source. Not guaranteed for accuracy or completeness.
