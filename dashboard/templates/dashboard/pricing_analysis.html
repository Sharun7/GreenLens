📖 How Mispricing is Defined

Mispricing = Actual Market Yield Spread − PCRS-Predicted Fair Spread.
The model computes a "fair" yield spread for each bond based on its Physical Climate Risk Score (PCRS), bond maturity, country risk, and project category using an OLS regression. If the actual market spread deviates beyond ±48 basis points from this prediction, the bond is flagged as mispriced — meaning the market is either overcharging or undercharging for the physical climate risk the project actually faces.

⚠️ Research Prototype Disclaimer: The PCRS model was trained on synthetic labels constructed from climate hazard indices, not historical bond default data (which is proprietary and not publicly available). This pricing gap analysis is a research-grade indicator of relative climate risk mispricing, not a certified financial rating. All outputs should be interpreted as signals for further due diligence, not investment advice.
