Trust Boundary
  • GreenLens is strongest when the question is physical verification, climate hazard, or pricing-vs-risk mismatch.
  • It is not a replacement for governance, labor, or broader company-behavior analysis.
  • The platform shows confidence ranges and keeps the research-only disclaimer visible.
  • Every score should be understood as explainable and auditable, not as a claim of perfect certainty.
