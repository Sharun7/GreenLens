## Revenue Model

**Recommended: Tiered SaaS + Enterprise Licensing**

GreenLens uses a **hybrid model** combining:
1. **Tiered SaaS subscriptions** (Academic, Professional, Business)
2. **Enterprise licensing** (Custom contracts, white-label, on-premise)

This approach maximizes:
- **Market coverage** — From individual researchers to sovereign wealth funds
- **Revenue predictability** — Recurring monthly subscriptions
- **Scalability** — Self-service for small customers, high-touch for enterprise

### Unique Value Propositions

#### 1. Satellite Verification
- **Problem:** All ESG providers rely on issuer self-reported data
- **Solution:** GreenLens uses ESA Sentinel-2 and NASA satellite imagery as primary evidence
- **Advantage:** Issuers cannot manipulate satellite pixels

#### 2. Full Explainability
- **Problem:** Bloomberg, MSCI are black boxes
- **Solution:** SHAP values show exact feature contributions
- **Advantage:** Every score is auditable and defensible

#### 3. Physical Climate Risk Focus
- **Problem:** Most ESG ratings focus on carbon emissions (transition risk)
- **Solution:** GreenLens focuses on physical climate risk (floods, heat, drought)
- **Advantage:** Addresses regulatory requirements (TCFD, SFDR)

#### 4. Independent Verification
- **Problem:** Rating agencies have conflicts of interest (issuer-pays model)
- **Solution:** GreenLens is investor-pays, no conflicts
- **Advantage:** Truly independent verification

## Known Limitations (Business Side)

### 1. Long Sales Cycle

**Problem:**
- Enterprise financial firms have 6-12 month procurement processes
- Multiple stakeholders (IT, Legal, Compliance, Finance)
- Security audits and vendor due diligence

**Impact:**
- Slow revenue growth in Year 1
- Cash flow challenges
- Need runway for 18-24 months

**Mitigation:**
- Focus on Professional/Business tiers first (shorter sales cycle)
- Offer annual prepayment discounts
- Bootstrap or raise seed funding

---

### 2. Legal Liability Risk

**Problem:**
- "GreenLens score was wrong, we lost money" — lawsuit possible
- Financial advice regulations (MiFID II, SEC)
- Data accuracy liability

**Impact:**
- Need professional indemnity insurance (€10,000-50,000/year)
- Legal disclaimers on every output
- Cannot market as "investment advice"

**Mitigation:**
- Clear disclaimers: "Research tool, not investment advice"
- Professional indemnity insurance
- Terms of Service with liability caps
- Regulatory compliance (not a registered investment advisor)

---

### 3. Regulatory Certification

**Problem:**
- SFDR certified data provider requires EU registration
- TCFD alignment requires third-party audit
- Time-consuming and expensive

**Impact:**
- Cannot claim "SFDR certified" without registration
- Enterprise customers may require certification
- Competitive disadvantage vs certified providers

**Mitigation:**
- Apply for SFDR data provider registration (6-12 months)
- Third-party audit by PwC or Deloitte (€50,000)
- Interim: "SFDR-aligned" instead of "SFDR-certified"

---

### 4. Competition from Well-Funded Startups

**Problem:**
- Clarity AI: $100M+ VC funding
- Persefoni: $100M+ VC funding
- Watershed: $70M+ VC funding
- GreenLens: Bootstrap (no VC funding)

**Impact:**
- Limited marketing budget
- Cannot outspend competitors
- Slower growth

**Mitigation:**
- Focus on differentiation (satellite verification)
- Academic credibility (research papers)
- Niche positioning (green bonds only, not all ESG)
- Organic growth through word-of-mouth
